❓ Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Reach out to us below.

How do I set up my Learning DNA?✚
Head to the assessment from your dashboard and answer a short set of questions about your class, board, subjects and study style. LearnMate AI uses this to personalize every lesson, quiz and revision set it builds for you.
Is LearnMate AI free to use?✚
Yes, schools and individual students can use the core Learning Canvas, quizzes and revision tools at no cost. No credit card is required to create an account.
Why does LearnMate AI build a full lesson instead of just answering my question?✚
Instead of a chat reply, every question generates a structured lesson page — diagrams, worked examples, a short quiz and notes — tailored to your Learning DNA, so you actually understand the concept rather than just get an answer.
Can I ask about topics outside school subjects?✚
No. LearnMate AI only responds to educational queries related to your curriculum. Anything outside academics is politely declined by design to keep the experience focused on learning.
How does Smart Revision decide what to show me?✚
It tracks the topics you've struggled with in quizzes and lessons, then resurfaces them at spaced intervals so weaker areas get more practice without you having to plan it yourself.
Where is my data stored?✚
Your progress, streaks, quiz scores and Learning DNA profile are stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to an external server.
How do I reset my progress or retake the assessment?✚
Go to Settings → Your data. You can reset progress (streaks, quiz scores, completed lessons) or reset your Learning DNA separately, whichever you need.
Which classes and boards are supported?✚
Class 1 through 12, across CBSE, ICSE and major state boards, with support for English and several regional languages.
