Questions

Everything you're wondering

Is LearnMate AI just another chatbot?
No. Asking a question doesn't open a chat thread — it generates a full structured lesson page (diagrams, examples, quizzes, notes) built around your Learning DNA.
Which classes and boards are supported?
Class 1 through 12, across CBSE, ICSE, and major state boards, in English and regional languages.
Can it answer non-study questions?
No — LearnMate AI only responds to educational queries. Anything outside academics is politely declined by design.
Is there a free plan for schools?
Yes, schools can onboard a full classroom to try the core canvas and adaptive quiz features at no cost.
